# Data methods
Inputs (sha256 in `data/source_manifest.json` and the asset): HC-256 (19,140 persons; 18,683 with PERWT24F>0), HC-254D (1,912 stays), HC-254E (4,351 ER visits), HC-254F (22,150 outpatient visits). Downloaded 2026-11-21 from meps.ahrq.gov.

**Person cells.** Persons with PERWT24F>0 are grouped by age band (AGELAST: 0-17, 18-44, 45-64, 65+) × payer × income band (POVCAT24: <200%FPL, 200-399, 400+). Payer is a *derived* hierarchy: INSCOV24==3 uninsured > MCREV24==1 Medicare > MCDEV24==1 Medicaid > PRVEV24==1 private > other. Per cell: weighted pop share and weighted mean ER (ERTOT24), outpatient (OPTOTV24) and inpatient (IPDIS24) events per person-year. Cells with n<50 use fallback rates (age×payer, then payer, then overall); flagged `sparse`.
**Event payments.** Facility payment (ER/OP/IPFXP24X), physician payment (…DXP24X) and facility self-pay (…FSF24X) per event, weighted by the event file's PERWT24F, joined to payer via DUPERSID (EVNTIDX asserted unique; join asserted non-duplicating). Exported as 21-point weighted quantile grids in integer cents plus the weighted mean of the top 5% (`tailMean`), which the simulator uses to preserve the mean in the heavy tail. Payer cells: n>=100 stand alone; 30-99 own distribution flagged low-sample; <30 pooled.
**Reconciliation.** Weighted event counts from event files equal person-file counts (ratio 1.000 for all three services). See `docs/DATA_REPORT.md`.
**Not done / limits.** No design-based variance estimates (VARSTR/VARPSU are carried in the inputs but not used). Inpatient is calibrated but not playable. Facility vs physician split is used as documented; ER visits that led to admission may carry their cost in the inpatient record. MEPS payments are not hospital revenue, charges or cost. Sentinel codes: all used payment/count variables were checked to have no negatives in positive-weight rows; checks assert this.

## Design-based variance and teaching statistics (final build)
`ratio_se` in `scripts/meps_common.py` computes ratio estimates with Taylor-linearised SEs using VARSTR (strata) and VARPSU (PSUs), with-replacement variance; domain estimates use an indicator in numerator and denominator over the full sample. Strata with a single PSU are skipped. Exported: payer/age shares, per-person-year rates, group-level rates by age, payer and income, any-ER-visit share, mean total expenditure by group (TOTEXP24), and spending concentration (point estimates). The quiz and coach read only these exported values.
